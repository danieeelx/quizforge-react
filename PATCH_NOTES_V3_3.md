# QuizForge v3.3

## Cleaner creation experience

- Split Create Study Set into dedicated **Upload PDF** and **Manual Builder** tabs.
- Removed the disabled AI Assistance panel from the creation screen.
- Removed the permanent OCR warning; the app now reports it only after detecting an image-only PDF.
- Removed Collapse All and the permanent Clear Draft footer link.
- Moved Clear Saved Draft into the page options menu.
- Added accurate draft/ready counts and disabled review until at least one manual question is ready.
- Added a sticky manual action bar with Add Question and Review Study Set.
- Replaced the exposed trash button and chevron with a compact three-dot menu.
- Hid Topic and Explanation under optional details.
- Added manual explanations to saved questions and answer review.
- Reduced hover effects, card glow, and unnecessary borders.
- Kept question and answer content in the reading font with normal capitalization.
